Chocolate Chip Cookies

These classic cookies combine a sweet dough with chocolate chips. They make a lovely treat to share with family and friends.

Preparation: 15 minutes | Baking: 10-12 minutes | Makes: About 12 cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup softened butter
  • 1/2 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup white sugar
  • 1 small egg
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C and line a baking tray with baking paper.
  2. Mix the softened butter and sugars until combined.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla extract. Mix until incorporated.
  4. Add the flour and baking soda. Stir until a dough forms.
  5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
  6. Place small portions of dough on the prepared tray, leaving space between each cookie.
  7. Bake for approximately 10–12 minutes, checking for lightly golden edges. Baking time may vary by oven.
  8. Let the cookies cool on the tray before transferring them.

Vanilla Cupcakes

Vanilla cupcakes are small, soft cakes with a delicate flavor. They can be decorated with frosting and sprinkles.

Preparation: 15 minutes | Baking: 18–22 minutes | Makes: 6 cupcakes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup softened butter
  • 1 egg
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Place cupcake liners in a muffin tray.
  2. Cream the butter and sugar together.
  3. Add the egg and vanilla extract. Mix until combined.
  4. In a separate bowl, combine the flour and baking powder.
  5. Add the dry ingredients to the butter mixture, alternating with the milk. Mix just until combined.
  6. Divide the batter among six cupcake liners, filling each about two-thirds full.
  7. Bake for approximately 18–22 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  8. Allow the cupcakes to cool completely before decorating.

For chocolate lovers

Chocolate Brownies

Brownies are rich chocolate baked goods that can have a fudgy or cake-like texture. This simple recipe is a great introduction to homemade chocolate desserts.

Preparation: 15 minutes | Baking: 20–25 minutes | Makes: 9 small squares

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/3 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup chocolate chips, optional

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C. Line a small square baking pan with baking paper.
  2. Combine the melted butter and sugar in a bowl.
  3. Add the eggs and vanilla extract. Mix until smooth.
  4. Sift in the cocoa powder, flour, and salt.
  5. Stir gently until the ingredients are combined. Fold in the chocolate chips if desired.
  6. Spread the batter evenly in the prepared baking pan.
  7. Bake for approximately 20–25 minutes. Check the center for your preferred brownie texture and doneness.
  8. Let the brownies cool before cutting them into squares.
